How to play

  1. Everyone starts with one card, year side up.
  2. On your turn, scan a new card with your phone camera.
  3. Watch the scene and place the card before, after or between the cards in your timeline.
  4. Flip it. If the year fits, the card stays in your timeline.
  5. Name the title and the characters in the scene to win a token. Spend 3 tokens to buy a free card.
  6. The first player with 10 cards wins.

For a harder game, turn on Sound only: you hear the scene but don’t see it.

Add a clip

Paste a link and the title and year are filled in from the video’s name. Check them, then add the characters.

To pick the start, play the preview, pause where the scene should begin, and click Start from here.

For a TV show, use the year that episode aired.

GitHub settings (needed to save from this page)

Create the token on GitHub: Settings → Developer settings → Personal access tokens → Fine-grained tokens → Generate new token. Under Repository access choose only this repository, and under Permissions set Contents to Read and write. The token is kept only in this browser, never on the site.

Print the cards

This page is open from a file on your computer, so phones can’t reach it. Host it first, then print from the hosted address.

To print only new cards, set the range to their numbers. Print double-sided, flip on the long edge, at 100% scale.
