How to play

  1. Everyone starts with one card, year side up.
  2. On your turn, scan a new card. The screen shows this turn’s bonus challenge.
  3. Watch the scene and place the card before, after or between the cards in your timeline.
  4. Flip it. If the year fits, the card stays in your timeline.
  5. Name the title and the bonus to win a token. Spend 3 tokens to buy a free card.
  6. The first player with 10 cards wins.

For a harder game, turn on Sound only: you hear the scene but don’t see it.
